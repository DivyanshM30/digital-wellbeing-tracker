Everything You Need

Real-Time Tracking

Automatically tracks which apps you're using and for how long. See exactly where your time goes with accurate, up-to-the-minute data.

Smart Insights

Discover patterns in your usage with K-Means clustering analysis. Get personalized insights about your digital habits and see when you're most productive.

Smart Reminders

Get gentle voice alerts and notifications when you're approaching your limits. Customize quiet hours so you're never interrupted during important moments.

Beautiful Charts

Visualize your usage with daily and weekly charts. See trends at a glance and understand your digital habits better with clear, easy-to-read graphs.

Lightweight & Fast

Privacy First

All your data stays on your device. No cloud sync, no tracking, no external servers. Your digital habits are yours alone.
